MedMNIST from scratch

MedMNIST from scratch: One small network, pointed at real medical images

Jaundel Simon

Research article (2026)

A two layer network written by hand in numpy, trained on chest X-rays, ultrasounds, blood smears and CT slices. The same gradient then shows what it looks at and breaks it with a perturbation you cannot see, before the network is federated across hospitals, pruned to a tenth of its weights, and trained again without backprop.

RISC-NPU

RISC-NPU: ISA-Integrated Neural Acceleration on a Custom FPGA CPU

Jaundel Simon

FPGA Thesis (2026)

A custom 32-bit RISC CPU extended with a tightly-coupled NPU datapath on an FPGA. Adding a MAC instruction to the ISA delivers a verified 12.3x reduction in cycle count over the software loop, the same principle independently discovered by ARM (VMLA), RISC-V ('V' extension), and Intel (AVX-512 VNNI). Every number is cycle-accurate from hardware simulation.
