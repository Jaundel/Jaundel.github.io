RISC-NPU

RISC-NPU: Neural Instructions as First-Class Citizens in a Custom ISA

Jaundel Simon

FPGA architecture thesis · Altera Cyclone IV E · Quartus Prime 25.1

A custom 32-bit RISC CPU extended with a tightly-coupled NPU datapath on an FPGA. Adding a MAC instruction to the ISA delivers a verified 12.3× reduction in cycle count over the software loop — the same principle independently discovered by ARM (VMLA), RISC-V ('V' extension), and Intel (AVX-512 VNNI). Every number is cycle-accurate from hardware simulation.
